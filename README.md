# John Heater

Accounting faculty at the University of Minnesota's Carlson School of Management. I study corporate disclosure, regulation, and the relation between accounting information and firm value.

[johnheater.com](https://www.johnheater.com/) is the home for my publications, working papers, and teaching information. This GitHub profile provides a starting point for research code, data workflows, and teaching tools as they become available.

## Current research

- **Detecting Conversation-Level Management Deception Using Response Latency** — response timing in earnings-call Q&A and its capital-market consequences.
- **Large short-position disclosures** — investor behavior and market responses to public disclosure in the UK.
- **Private-information returns and mutual funds** — validating measures of private information using fund performance and trading.
- **Earnings calls versus 10-K MD&A** — semantic differences in topic coverage and framing, and their implications for investors.

## Teaching tools

For **ACCT 2051: Introduction to Financial Reporting**, I develop tools that help students connect financial statements and disclosure to economic decisions. Projects include disclosure analysis, comparisons of earnings calls and MD&A, and financial-statement comparisons across firms.
